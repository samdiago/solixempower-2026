# SOLIXEmpower 2026 – San Diego

SOLIXEmpower 2026 is an enterprise technology event bringing together data, AI, and technology leaders to explore the future of Enterprise AI, Data Governance, Cloud Data Management, and Agentic AI.

## Event Details

- **Event:** SOLIXEmpower 2026
- **Date:** October 28–30, 2026
- **Location:** UC San Diego, San Diego, California

## Key Topics

SOLIXEmpower 2026 explores important areas of enterprise technology, including:

- Enterprise AI
- AI-Ready Data
- Enterprise Data Governance
- Cloud Data Management
- Application Knowledge Graphs (AKG)
- Generative AI
- Agentic AI
- AI Governance and Trust
- AI in Healthcare and Life Sciences
- Enterprise AI Agents

## What to Expect

The event features keynotes, expert panels, workshops, demonstrations, networking opportunities, and discussions focused on practical enterprise AI adoption.

Sessions explore how organizations can connect trusted enterprise data, governance, context, and AI to support digital transformation.

## AI and Data Management

As enterprises adopt generative AI and AI agents, trusted and governed data is becoming increasingly important. Organizations need reliable data, context, security, and governance to support enterprise AI initiatives.

SOLIXEmpower 2026 brings these topics together through discussions around enterprise data management, AI governance, semantic context, and agentic AI.
